### Slide 2 – The Problem (Start from Pain)
- Learning is fragmented across PDFs, videos, apps — no coherent journey.
- Content is static — hard to update, hard to personalize, hard to practice.
- Teachers are overloaded — cannot create high-quality interactive material for every topic.
- Institutions lack a feedback loop — they can’t see where students struggle or why.

### Slide 3 – Pain Points by Stakeholder
Students  
- Passive learning, low engagement, low retention  
- Hard to connect “formula” with intuition and real-world meaning  
- Practice is not adaptive; feedback is late or generic

Teachers & Content Teams  
- Repetitive preparation; low reuse across classes, levels, and languages  
- Manual authoring is slow; quality varies; maintenance is painful  
- No scalable way to run “1:1 tutoring” for everyone

Schools, Publishers, Platforms  
- Slow and expensive production cycles  
- Limited learning analytics and weak evidence of impact  
- Hard to standardize quality across courses and providers

### Slide 4 – Our Solution in 3 Pillars
1) **Make each question a step‑by‑step story, not a cold final answer**  
   - Turn every exercise into an animated solution path  
   - Students see how to think, not just what the result is  
   - Focus on reasoning steps, key transitions, and “why this move”

2) **When students get stuck and don’t know what to ask, the system behaves like a teacher**  
   - Proactive questioning, hints, and error‑specific nudges  
   - Corrects misunderstandings along the way instead of dumping the full solution  
   - Encourages the student to try, reflect, and adjust

3) **The system learns the student over time**  
   - Remembers where the student frequently makes mistakes and progresses slowly  
   - Detects patterns in preferred learning styles and support needs  
   - Becomes more personalized the more it is used, instead of starting from zero each time

### Slide 5 – The Shift: From Content to an AI Teacher
eduNest turns learning content into an **AI Teacher** built on these 3 pillars:
- Generates lessons, explanations, and step‑by‑step solution flows for each question  
- Guides learners with questions, hints, and formative feedback when they are stuck  
- Uses accumulated learning signals to adapt difficulty, pacing, and teaching style

### Slide 6 – What is eduNest?
An AI-native platform to **generate, deliver, and improve** interactive learning modules:
- Author once → deliver across devices  
- Personalize for language, level, and learning pace  
- Continuously improve using learning analytics

From content usage to measurable learning outcomes:

- Identify where learners get stuck and which misconceptions dominate
- Compare cohorts, classes, and content versions to quantify impact
- Use analytics to drive iteration: improve prompts, content, and curriculum decisions

### Slide 2 – 问题（从痛点开始）
- 学习体验碎片化：教材/PDF/视频/App 分散，缺乏完整学习旅程
- 内容静态：难更新、难个性化、难形成有效练习
- 老师/教研压力大：无法为每个知识点持续产出高质量互动内容
- 缺少反馈闭环：机构看不到学生卡点与原因，难以持续改进

### Slide 3 – 按角色拆分痛点
学生  
- 被动学习，参与度低、遗忘快  
- 公式难以与直觉、真实情境建立联系  
- 练习不自适应，反馈滞后或泛泛而谈

教师与教研/内容团队  
- 备课重复劳动，复用率低  
- 人工创作慢、质量不稳定、维护成本高  
- 缺少可规模化的“1 对 1 辅导层”

学校/出版机构/平台  
- 内容生产周期长、成本高  
- 学习数据与学习分析不足，难以证明效果  
- 难以跨课程/机构统一质量标准

### Slide 4 – 整体解决方案：三个核心方向
① **把每一道题，变成“一步一步动起来的解题过程”**  
   - 每道题都变成一个“会动的解题故事”，而不是一行冷冰冰的答案  
   - 学生看到的是思路、关键转折点和“为什么这么想”，而不是只看结果  
   - 重点呈现推理链路、拆分步骤和关键中间结论

② **学生做题卡住、甚至不知道该问什么时，系统像老师一样追问和引导**  
   - 主动抛出问题、给出提示和针对性纠错，而不是一上来就给全解  
   - 根据错误类型给出不同提问方式和引导语  
   - 鼓励学生先尝试、再反思、再调整，而不是被动抄答案

③ **系统会长期“记住这个学生”，越用越懂他**  
   - 记录学生容易错的知识点、进步最慢的环节和常见误区  
   - 识别他更适合什么学习方式（多举例？多练习？多视觉化？）  
   - 每次练习都在前一次的基础上调整，而不是每次都从零开始

### Slide 5 – 关键转变：从“内容”到“AI 老师”
基于这三个方向，eduNest 把内容升级为 **AI 老师**：
- 围绕题目和知识点生成分步讲解与练习链路  
- 学生卡住时，通过追问、提示和过程纠偏来“带着做题”  
- 利用长期积累的学习信号，调整难度、节奏和讲解方式

### Slide 6 – 什么是 eduNest？
一个用来 **生成、交付、持续改进** 学习模块的 AI 原生平台：
- 一次创作，多端交付  
- 按语言、水平与学习节奏个性化  
- 用学习分析驱动持续迭代

### Slide 7 – 核心能力（产品层）
AI 老师体验  
- 分阶段学习路径：引入 → 探索 → 练习 → 总结

多语言交付

### Slide 8 – Learning Analytics（学习分析：反馈闭环）
从“用没用”到“学会没学会”：
- 记录细粒度学习事件（浏览、作答、错误类型、耗时、提示使用等）
- 定位卡点与主要误区：学生为什么错、错在哪一步
- 支持班级/机构/版本对比，量化内容效果与学习增益
- 以数据驱动迭代：优化提示词、内容结构与课程决策
